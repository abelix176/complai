MiFID II, Directive 2014/65/EU, Article 24(3) (verbatim):

"Member States shall ensure that all information, including marketing
communications, addressed by the investment firm to clients or potential
clients shall be fair, clear and not misleading. Marketing communications
shall be clearly identifiable as such."

---

Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/565, Article 44(2) (verbatim,
selected sub-points):

Note: the information referred to is that covered by Article 44(1) -
"all information [investment firms] address to, or disseminate in such a
way that it is likely to be received by, retail or professional clients or
potential retail or professional clients, including marketing
communications."

Article 44(2)(b):
"the information is accurate and always gives a fair and prominent
indication of any relevant risks when referencing any potential benefits
of an investment service or financial instrument"

Article 44(2)(c):
"uses a font size in the indication of relevant risks that is at least
equal to the predominant font size used throughout the information
provided, as well as a layout ensuring such indication is prominent"

Article 44(2)(d):
"the information is sufficient for, and presented in a way that is likely
to be understood by, the average member of the group to whom it is
directed, or by whom it is likely to be received"

Article 44(2)(e):
"the information does not disguise, diminish or obscure important items,
statements or warnings"
